Where nurses fit in health tech

A few of the most common paths nurses take. Tap a filter to narrow it down.

Data & informatics

Clinical Informatics Specialist

Bridge clinicians and EHR systems — optimize workflows, build order sets, and train staff.

  • Workflow design
  • EHR (Epic, Cerner)
  • Training
Product & design

Clinical Product Manager

Shape what gets built. Turn frontline problems into product requirements teams can ship.

  • Prioritization
  • User stories
  • Stakeholders
Product & design

UX Researcher

Interview clinicians and patients, run usability tests, and make tools actually usable at 3 a.m.

  • Interviewing
  • Usability testing
  • Synthesis
Clinical-facing

Clinical Implementation Specialist

Roll out new technology at hospitals and clinics, and make sure it sticks with the care team.

  • Change management
  • Go-live support
  • Education
Clinical-facing

Clinical Success Manager

Partner with health system customers, drive adoption, and bring their feedback to the product team.

  • Relationships
  • Adoption metrics
  • Advocacy
Data & informatics

Clinical AI / Content Specialist

Validate clinical logic, review AI outputs for safety, and author evidence-based content.

  • Clinical validation
  • Evidence review
  • Safety
