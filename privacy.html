Privacy

How we use your details.

Short and simple: we only use what you share to help you move into health tech.

What we collect

When you fill out a form on this site, we receive what you type into it. That's usually your name and email, and anything optional you choose to add, such as your phone number, location, LinkedIn profile, nursing background, the roles you're interested in, and any notes.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you and follow up on your request.
  • To suggest resources, roles or events that fit what you told us.
  • To introduce you to a nurse working in the role you're curious about, only if you asked for an introduction.
  • To add you to the Nurse2Tech WhatsApp community, only if you ticked that box.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your details.
  • We never share your details with employers or recruiters without your permission.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies on this site. Our visitor statistics are anonymous and cookie-free, and only count page visits.

How it's handled

When you submit a form, your details are sent securely to the Nurse2Tech inbox by email. Like most websites, this site uses trusted third-party services for hosting, fonts, form delivery and simple visitor statistics, which may receive standard technical information (such as your browser type) when a page loads. They don't receive anything you type unless you submit a form.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the details you've shared, or to stop contacting you. Just email connect@nurse2tech.com and we'll take care of it.
