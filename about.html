About us

Nurses belong in health tech.

Nurse2Tech helps nurses turn clinical experience into careers building the tools that shape patient care.

Our mission

Health tech is built for clinicians, but too often without them. Nurses understand workflows, patient safety and the realities of care better than anyone, yet the path from the bedside into tech is unclear.

We make that path visible. We show nurses where they fit, help them close skill gaps, reframe their experience, and connect them with nurses who've already made the move.

What we do

Show real roles

Clear, honest looks at the health tech jobs nurses actually get hired for.

Find the gaps

Compare your skills with real job postings so you know exactly what to learn next.

Reframe experience

Translate clinical work into the language tech teams hire for.

Make connections

Introduce nurses to nurses already working in the roles they want.

What we believe

Clinical experience is a tech skill.Assessment, prioritization and communication under pressure are exactly what product teams need.
Nobody should figure it out alone.The fastest way into health tech is through people who've done it.
Better tools mean better care.When nurses help build technology, patients and clinicians both benefit.
